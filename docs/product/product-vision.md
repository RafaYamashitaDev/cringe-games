# Cringe Games Product Vision

Cringe Games is intended to be a collection of free, clean, fast,
and enjoyable games.

FreeCell is the first game in the collection.

At least two additional games are planned for the future, but their
selection, requirements, and implementation are deliberately outside
the current FreeCell development scope.

The immediate objective is to develop and release FreeCell while
establishing an engineering workflow that can later support additional
games without introducing speculative complexity today.
